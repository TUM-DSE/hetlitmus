# About

HetLitmus was developed by Nguyen Le in the bachelor's thesis *Generation of CPU-GPU
Heterogeneous Litmus Tests for Real Hardware* at the
[Chair of Distributed Systems and Operating Systems](https://dse.in.tum.de/), Technical University
of Munich (TUM), 2026. The thesis was supervised by Dr. Anatole Lefort and examined by
Prof. Dr. Pramod Bhatotia.
